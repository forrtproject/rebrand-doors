    FORRT AI in Metascience Online Conference

    On this page
    • Programme
    • Recordings
    • Code of conduct
    • Questions

    How is AI changing the way research is done, checked and understood? The FORRT AI in Metascience Online Conference spent three days on that question, with 35+ talks and workshops from people who are studying it or building with it. Most talks are now on the FORRT YouTube channel, one playlist per day.

    When28–30 September 2026

    Tuesday 29 SeptemberReplications and reproductions

    Replications and reproductions are key to credible science, yet hard to do and hard to find. Can AI help with both, and what are the risks of handing the work to machines?

    All Tuesday recordings on YouTube

    09:30Symposium: Can AI Help Us Understand How Findings Move from Animal Studies to Human Studies?

    Four talks, mostly by early-career researchers, on tracing the biomedical evidence trail across publications, trial registries and regulatory documents. Chaired by Benjamin Ineichen.

    13:20Getting our hands dirty: experimenting with agentic reproductions and robustness checks

    Three coding agents get the same prompt on two well-known cases: reproduce the result, then test how robust it is. While they run, we look at how coding agents work and how to set up tasks so that their output can be checked. Then we go through what came back. No prior experience needed.

    Recordings

    The talks whose speakers agreed to publication are on the FORRT YouTube channel, with one playlist per day: Monday, Tuesday and Wednesday. Talks without a Watch link in the programme were not recorded or are not public. For two of the Wednesday workshops, the playlist has a shorter version.

    Code of conduct

    The conference followed the FORRT code of conduct. To raise a concern about anything that happened at the conference, email the organiser at l.wallrich@bbk.ac.uk. If you would rather not come to us, report it to the FORRT ethics committee, either by contacting any of its members or through the anonymous reporting form. FORRT’s confidential advisor is there if you would like to talk something through first.

    Questions

    Email the organiser, Lukas Wallrich, at l.wallrich@bbk.ac.uk, or message him on the FORRT Slack.
