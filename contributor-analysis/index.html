    FORRT Contributor Analyses

    How many people contribute to FORRT, where they work and what they do, and how shared projects connect them.

    As of 10 June 2026, FORRT has a total of 109 completed or ongoing projects and support teams, with a total of 647 contributors. There is an average (mean) of 2.44 contributions per person across all FORRT projects, and the average number of contributors per project is 14. You can see the full list of FORRT contributors and their individual contributions here.
